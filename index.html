Methodology & Logic

1. Capacity (Max 35 pts)

Measures the company's ability to sustain operations based on cash flow (Est. Runway).

IF Runway ≤ 6 mos → +35 pts
IF Runway ≥ 24 mos → +0 pts
ELSE → 35 - ((Runway - 6) / 18) * 35

2. Character (Max 30 pts)

Evaluates organizational stability and culture through Employee Attrition rates.

IF Attrition ≥ 20% → +30 pts
IF Attrition ≤ 5% → +0 pts
ELSE → ((Attrition - 5) / 15) * 30

3. Capital (Max 20 pts)

Assesses financial backing and primary revenue generation capabilities.

IF Revenue ≤ $2M → +20 pts
IF Revenue ≥ $20M → +0 pts
ELSE → 20 - ((Revenue - 2) / 18) * 20

4. Conditions & Collateral (Max 15 pts)

The AI assesses market volatility, recent funding, and leadership changes to output a qualitative index.

AI Volatility Index = 0 to 15 pts
